A scene loads by bare name (pt render cornell_restir_gi) or by path.

What each scene is for#

analytic_spheres#

The analytic validation scene.

Configured for :uniform at :M 1 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

bias_demo#

The scene that makes 1/M reuse visibly biased.

Configured for :restir-di at :M 8 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

cornell#

The Cornell box, authored in the EDN DSL.

Configured for :uniform at :M 1 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

cornell_gi#

The Cornell box again, with one indirect bounce.

Configured for :uniform at :M 1 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

cornell_restir#

The Cornell box with ReSTIR DI.

Configured for :restir-di at :M 32 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

cornell_restir_gi#

The Cornell box with ReSTIR GI: one indirect bounce, resampled.

Configured for :restir-gi at :M 1 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

gi_bias_demo#

The scene that makes 1/M path reuse visibly biased.

Configured for :restir-gi at :M 1 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

many_lights#

Many scattered lights — the regime RIS and ReSTIR actually exist for.

Configured for :restir-di at :M 8 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.

reproject_demo#

The scene that makes 1/M temporal reuse measurably biased.

Configured for :restir-di at :M 8 with target :unshadowed-cosine and :bias :mis.
