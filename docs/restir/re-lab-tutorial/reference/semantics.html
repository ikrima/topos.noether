Semantics of the estimator

What the estimator is, stated precisely enough for a reimplementation to satisfy.

Source: docs/architecture/semantics.mdinformation-oriented

What the estimator is, stated precisely enough to check. This is the document a reimplementation must satisfy; reference/ says where each piece lives and porting-to-racket.md says what to do with it.

Notation: p̂ is the target function, M a confidence, W a contribution weight, mᵢ an MIS weight, J a Jacobian. Scene and target mode are suppressed where constant.

1. The compositional structure#

Eight layers, each defined in terms of the one above.

#ObjectDefinition
1Payload yLightSample (a point on a light) or PathSample (a path suffix through one reconnection vertex)
2Reservoir(y, w_sum, M, W) ∈ (Payload ∪ {⊥}) × ℝ≥0 × ℝ≥0 × ℝ≥0
3Stream(Reservoir, Hit) — a reservoir plus the shading point that defines its target
4ReuseDomain(p̂, J) — the only payload-dependent data in the algebra
5Combinationcombine : Hit × [Stream] × [u] × Rule × ReuseDomain → Reservoir
6Passesinitial+temporal, spatial, resolve — the same signatures for both payloads
7Frame loopcarried state (reservoir_prev, hits_prev, cam_prev)
8Determinismrand : (pixel, sample, bounce, dim, seed) → [0,1), a pure hash

Layer 4 is the whole trick. Exactly two inhabitants exist, and everything else is written once.

2. Payloads and measure#

Both payloads store a world-space point. They differ in which measure their density lives in, and that difference — not the storage — is what decides whether reuse needs a Jacobian.

LightSamplePathSample
Storesp, wi, dist, radiance, pdf_area, pdf_solid_angle, light_id, cos_lightp (=x₂), n (=n₂), radiance (=L(x₂→x₁)), pdf_solid_angle, prim_id
Density inarea at the sampled pointsolid angle at the vertex it was traced from (x₁)

DI_DOMAIN.jacobian ≡ 1 not because the payload is a world-space point — the GI payload is one too — but because a light sample's pdf is stored in area measure, which is view-independent.

Re-deriving wi and dist from a stored point is exact (light_sample_at), which is what makes that identity hold rather than approximately hold.

3. Streaming RIS#

A reservoir summarizes an arbitrarily long candidate stream in O(1).

Absorb (Chao's weighted reservoir sampling), with an explicit confidence increment:

absorb((y, s, M, W), y', w, m, u) =
    (y,  s,      M + m, W)                     if w ≤ 0 or w = NaN
    (y', s + w,  M + m, W)                     if u < w / (s + w)
    (y,  s + w,  M + m, W)                     otherwise

update(r, y, w, u) = absorb(r, y, w, 1, u) — a fresh candidate carries confidence 1.

Property (survivor law). After streaming candidates with weights wᵢ, the held sample is yᵢ with probability wᵢ / Σⱼ wⱼ. With wᵢ = p̂(yᵢ)/p(yᵢ) the survivor is distributed ∝ p̂.

Finalize — two forms, and which one is correct depends on whether the mᵢ have already been paid:

finalize_ris(r, p̂(y)) :  W = w_sum / (M · p̂(y))       candidates i.i.d. from one stream
finalize_mis(r, p̂(y)) :  W = w_sum / p̂(y)             streams already carry mᵢ

Property. finalize_ris at M = 1 collapses to 1 / p_source, so RIS is a strict generalization of plain importance sampling. Worth asserting before trusting larger M.

Trap. No 1/M in finalize_mis: the mᵢ already sum to one over the contributing streams, so dividing by M as well double-normalizes and darkens the result. In the kernels the choice is carried by an explicit mis_merged flag rather than inferred from M, because "no history was found" and "history was found and weighted" are different states that can leave M looking the same.

4. The reuse domain#

ReuseDomain = (p̂, J)
  p̂ : Scene × Hit × Payload × TargetMode → ℝ≥0
  J : Scene × Payload × Hit_from × Hit_to → ℝ≥0        as |d(to) / d(from)|

Transformation law. A density transforms with the inverse Jacobian; a contribution weight, being a reciprocal density, with the forward one. Hence the two directions appear in different places and are not interchangeable:

SiteCallWhy
MIS denominatorJ(y, y_hit → stream i)carry y's density into stream i's domain, so the balance heuristic compares like with like
AbsorbJ(y, stream i → q)carry stream i's W into the merge target's domain

The two inhabitants:

DI_DOMAIN = (phat_at,    λ_.1.0)
GI_DOMAIN = (gi_phat_at, reconnection_jacobian)

Target modes#

Modep̂
UNSHADOWEDLe · cos_light / d²
UNSHADOWED_COSINEf · Le · G, unshadowed — the default
FULLwith visibility; one ray per candidate

Property. p̂ must be non-zero wherever the contribution is non-zero. A target that is zero where the integrand is not is biased, full stop. This is the whole content of the grazing-epsilon finding: an epsilon added to decide whether a shift exists also zeroed p̂ for the canonical stream's own sample.

5. Reconnection — the only non-trivial shift#

The shift mapping is the identity on the sample and non-trivial only on the measure. Both payloads store world-space points; nothing is re-traced.

For a stored vertex x₂ seen from shading points r and q:

dω = cos(θ₂) · dA(x₂) / d²                     (area ↔ solid angle)

J(r → q) = |dω_q / dω_r| = (cos_q / d_q²) · (d_r² / cos_r)

with both cosines at x₂, never at x₁ — the measure conversion foreshortens the area element.

Measure choice is exclusive. Two equivalent formulations; mixing them double-counts:

ParameterizationIntegrandpdf inJ
area at x₂f · G · Larea1
solid anglef · cos θ₁ · Lsolid anglethe ratio above

This implementation uses the solid-angle form. G already contains the conversion the Jacobian applies, so gi_contribution returns f · cos(θ₁) · L, not f · G · L.

Three deliberate non-choices, each of which would be a plausible mistake:

  • No clamp on J. A grazing reconnection is a large finite number — the correct answer, and a genuine firefly. Clamping silently rewrites the estimator.
  • No grazing threshold in p̂. Refusing a shift on a threshold is unbiased (the stream leaves the MIS denominator as well as the sum, exactly as a geometric-gate rejection does). Zeroing p̂ is not.
  • Strict sidedness, no epsilon. Only DIST_EPSILON = 1e-6 survives, guarding arithmetic with no answer.

Precondition. Reconnection is legal only because every BSDF here is Lambertian, so L(x₂→x₁) is view-independent. This is the first thing that breaks with a glossy material.

6. MIS weights#

For a fixed sample y, define each stream's unnormalized density expressed in y's domain:

densityᵢ(y) = Mᵢ · p̂ᵢ(y) · J(y, y_hit → hitᵢ)

Three rules:

Rulemᵢ(y)CostDepends on y?
NAIVEMᵢ / Σⱼ MⱼO(1)no
MIS (generalized balance, Talbot)densityᵢ(y) / Σⱼ densityⱼ(y)O(N) evals per y, O(N²) per mergeyes
PAIRWISE_MISeach non-canonical weighted against the canonical only: mᵢ = (1/K)·a/(a+b), m_c = (1/K)·Σ b/(a+b)O(N)yes

The proof obligation#

∀ y in the combined support :   Σᵢ mᵢ(y) = 1

All three rules satisfy it. NAIVE satisfies it too — which is exactly why the partition of unity is necessary but not sufficient. NAIVE's bias comes from mᵢ ignoring y entirely: a sample that some stream could never have produced still pays that stream's full share.

Quantifier trap. The estimator uses mᵢ(yᵢ) — each stream weighted at its own sample — and those do not sum to one, nor need they. The obligation is over a fixed y. mis_weights_at computes the former; _mis_weights the latter. Any formalization must keep these apart; this is the distinction a judgment form makes unambiguous and a docstring does not.

Two collapses worth knowing#

  • Same shading point ⇒ balance ≡ naive, exactly. If all streams share a hit then p̂ᵢ(y) = p̂(y) for all i and J = 1, so densityᵢ(y) = Mᵢ · p̂(y) and the ratio is Mᵢ/ΣMⱼ. Consequence: temporal reuse without reprojection is unbiased under every rule, and the :bias toggle is provably inert on that pass. Measured: the three weightings agree to f32 rounding. Bias in reuse comes from combining different domains.
  • Two streams ⇒ pairwise ≡ balance, term for term. 1/K' = 1. Measured bit-identical, which is why the temporal kernel has no third branch.

7. Combination#

combine(q, streams, us, rule, dom) =
  let m = mis_weights(streams, rule, dom)
      r = fold over (sᵢ, mᵢ, uᵢ):
            if sᵢ.y = ⊥ :  absorb(r, ⊥, 0, sᵢ.M, 0)          -- confidence still counts
            else         :  absorb(r, sᵢ.y, wᵢ, sᵢ.M, uᵢ)
              where wᵢ = p̂(q, sᵢ.y) · sᵢ.W · J(sᵢ.y, sᵢ.hit → q) · (mᵢ if rule ≠ NAIVE else sᵢ.M)
  in finalize(r, p̂(q, r.y), ris if rule = NAIVE else mis)

Three properties this must preserve:

  1. Empty streams still carry confidence. absorb(r, ⊥, 0, sᵢ.M, 0) — and the caller must not filter them out either. Both halves are required; see 07-invariants.
  2. The Jacobian is not an MIS weight and does not cancel against one. NAIVE still pays it. Leaving it out is a second, independent bias.
  3. E[w_sum] is preserved by a reuse pass. This is the estimator's own internal quantity and the decisive diagnostic — it was E[w_sum], read straight out of the reservoir buffers, that identified the empty-stream defect when the image could not.

8. Determinism#

rand : (pixel, sample, bounce, dim, seed) → [0, 1)

A pure hash. No stream state anywhere; ti.random() is banned. The dimension layout is a contract between core/rng.py and kernels/_common.py (02-core).

Two laws the layout must satisfy:

  • Injectivity. No two distinct draws share (bounce, dim) for the same (pixel, sample). Blocks whose width is runtime-scaled (M · CAND_STRIDE) make a fixed offset collide for some M, invisibly.
  • Vertex-0 stability. Vertex 0 keeps BOUNCE_SHADE at any path depth, so L(k bounces) − L(1 bounce) is the indirect term exactly, per sample. Every GI correctness claim rests on this.

One alias is deliberate and documented: GI candidate 0's (u, v) are bsdf_dim(0, ·), which makes ReSTIR GI at M=1 with reuse off draw the same numbers as the two-bounce uniform path tracer, so the two can be compared per sample. Their outputs differ by about eps / d, the bounce ray's origin offset. The price is GI_BOUNCE_EXACTLY_TWO.

9. Frame semantics#

Carried state: (reservoir_prev, hits_prev, cam_prev).

frame(s):
  hits        ← trace primary rays for sample s
  res_temporal ← for every pixel: initial+temporal(hits, reservoir_prev, hits_prev)
  res_curr     ← for every pixel: spatial(hits, res_temporal)
  L            ← for every pixel: resolve(hits, res_curr) + emitted + (NEE at x₁ if GI)
  reservoir_prev ← (res_temporal if GI else res_curr)
  hits_prev      ← hits

Four ordering laws:

  1. Phase separation. Every pixel completes the temporal pass before any pixel starts the spatial pass. Otherwise each pixel samples an arbitrary mixture of updated and stale neighbours. The graph enforces this structurally by having spatial read reservoir_temporal and write reservoir_curr.
  2. History pairing. reservoir_prev[i] was built at hits_prev[i]; they roll together.
  3. Roll policy is payload-dependent. GI rolls the pre-spatial reservoir. Rolling the post-spatial one lets a sample travel to a neighbour and return as a second stream offering the same draw to the same merge; the balance heuristic adds one denominator term per stream, so a draw held by k streams has its probability counted k times, every mᵢ comes out too small, and the estimator loses energy. Measured: 31% distinct vertices, 3.2 copies per sample, energy 0.955 at m_cap 20 → 0.990 with duplication forced to exactly 1.00 and the m_cap scaling gone.
  4. No history is -1, never a fallback. reprojected_pixel returning -1 means the stream is absent, not clamped to a neighbour.

Warm-up is part of the semantics, not the measurement. A ReSTIR run begins as something close to a path tracer and becomes the estimator under test over roughly m_cap frames. The steady-state estimator is what any claim is about.

10. What is still open#

With the duplicated-sample fix in place and warm-up discarded, path reuse is still short of the converged indirect term, and the amount is a property of how far reuse reaches rather than of the weighting. On gi_bias_demo @128px, indirect energy across uniform / no reuse / temporal only / spatial only / both:

mis0.99961.00000.97400.92000.8960
naive—1.00000.97400.85210.8463

Pinned down: exact without reuse on both scenes, so it enters through a reuse pass; temporal-only is weighting-insensitive (agreeing to four decimals, as §6's collapse says it must) and still 2.6% short, so it is not the 1/M defect; MIS recovers 0.06 of the 0.16 that 1/M loses spatially and leaves 0.08; the deficit scales with the spatial radius (r = 4/8/16/32 → 0.9529/0.9287/0.9002/0.8960).

Eliminated: a resolution artifact (64/128/256px → 0.898/0.896/0.910 at fixed radius), and the measurement itself (uniform control 1.0006 ± 0.0002; no-reuse identity 1.0000).

The lead. At radius 4 the ordering inverts — naive 0.985 against mis 0.953. A weighting meant to add energy back leaving less of it than the biased one is the shape of an over-large MIS denominator: the same family as the two defects already found, where a stream was counted that could not have produced the sample, and where a sample was counted twice. That is a lead, not a conclusion.

Any reimplementation will reproduce this deficit. It is a property of the estimator as specified here, not of the Python.
